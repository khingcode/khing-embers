Mum’s Sunday soup
A note from the maker

I built this because of my mum’s fridge.

Growing up, her fridge was a magic trick. Odds and ends went in on Monday, and by Sunday a pot of something wonderful came out. She never followed a recipe. She just looked.

When I moved out, I couldn’t do it. I threw away so much food that I started keeping a tally on a sticky note. It was embarrassing. Ember is my attempt to put her eyes in your pocket.

Questions, answered.

Do I need to scan every item?

Nope. A single photo of each shelf is enough, or tap the handful of things you have. Ember is happy to guess, and you can correct it.

What if I’m missing an ingredient?

Ember only suggests dinners you can make now, and flags one swap or missing staple when it’s close, like “no cream? Use yogurt.”

Does it handle allergies and diets?

Yes. Set them once for each person in your household and they’re respected on every suggestion.

Is it really free?

The Pantry plan is free forever. Hearth pays for the shared-fridge servers and keeps us independent.
